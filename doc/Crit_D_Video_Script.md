# Criterion D Video Script

Target length: about 5 minutes 30 seconds

## Recording rules
- Record only the finished product.
- Use prepared demo data.
- Cut out slow typing and waiting.
- Keep the cursor movements deliberate.
- Use short spoken narration aligned to the success criteria.

## Opening (0:00-0:20)
**Show:** Launch the app on iPad home screen, then open the app.

**Say:**
> This video demonstrates the completed volunteer management system for Ulink College of Shanghai. I will show how the product satisfies each success criterion, using the native iPad client and the organiser management workflows.

---

## 1. Registration and login (0:20-0:50)
**Show:** Auth screen, registration form with school email, name, class, avatar, then sign in.

**Say:**
> First, the system allows volunteers and organisers to register with their school email, real name, class, and avatar. After registration, users can sign in securely with their account credentials.

---

## 2. Authority-based access control (0:50-1:15)
**Show:** Volunteer account view with normal tabs. Then switch to organiser account showing the Manage tab and organiser controls. Briefly show admin panel user management.

**Say:**
> Second, authority-based access control separates volunteers, organisers, and administrators. Volunteers can browse and apply, organisers can create activities and confirm attendance, and administrators manage users and permissions through the web panel.

---

## 3. Activity publication (1:15-1:45)
**Show:** Open organiser workspace, tap Create Activity, show form fields: title, date, location, duration, capacity, short description, full description.

**Say:**
> Third, organisers can publish activities with all required fields, including title, date, location, capacity, duration, and description. These details are structured to match the client’s real workflow.

---

## 4. Activity appears in the feed (1:45-2:00)
**Show:** Return to feed and show the published activity card visible in the volunteer feed.

**Say:**
> After publication, the activity appears in the volunteer feed, where students can compare opportunities quickly and open the full detail page.

---

## 5. Edit and cancel activity (2:00-2:25)
**Show:** Open activity management, edit an activity, then show state controls such as Recruiting, Start, End, Cancel.

**Say:**
> Fourth, organisers can edit published activities and control their lifecycle. Changes propagate through the shared system so volunteers do not rely on outdated spreadsheet or chat information.

---

## 6. Apply, approve, and capacity protection (2:25-2:55)
**Show:** Volunteer applies to an activity. Switch to organiser participant controls and show approve/reject controls. If possible, show a full activity state or mention prepared full-capacity test data.

**Say:**
> Fifth, volunteers can apply to activities, and organisers can approve or reject them. Capacity protection is enforced on the server, so overbooking is prevented even when multiple users apply at the same time.

---

## 7. Activity messaging channel (2:55-3:20)
**Show:** Open comments or team chat and scroll existing messages.

**Say:**
> Sixth, each activity has a scoped communication space. This keeps logistics and updates attached to the correct event instead of being scattered across personal chats.

---

## 8. Participation confirmation and records (3:20-3:50)
**Show:** In organiser workflow, confirm attendance for a participant. Then switch to Records screen showing confirmed hours and record state.

**Say:**
> Seventh, after an activity finishes, organisers can confirm attendance. The participation record then moves into its completed state, and the confirmed duration becomes part of the volunteer’s official record.

---

## 9. Leaderboard (3:50-4:10)
**Show:** Open leaderboard screen.

**Say:**
> Eighth, the leaderboard ranks volunteers by confirmed hours. This ranking is derived from participation records, so it stays consistent with the actual attendance data.

---

## 10. Export workflow (4:10-4:35)
**Show:** In organiser workspace, trigger Export Hours and show generated CSV preview.

**Say:**
> Ninth, the system can generate a structured export of confirmed volunteer hours for the school’s ISMAS reporting workflow. This removes the manual copy-paste process described by the client.

---

## 11. iPad compatibility (4:35-5:00)
**Show:** Rotate between portrait and landscape on key screens such as authentication, feed, and records.

**Say:**
> Tenth, the application remains usable on iPad in both portrait and landscape orientations. Buttons, forms, and navigation remain accessible without layout breakage.

---

## Closing (5:00-5:20)
**Show:** Return to feed or organiser workspace.

**Say:**
> Overall, the final product centralises volunteering, communication, participation records, and reporting into one maintainable system, directly addressing the client’s original problems with fragmented announcements, overbooking, manual hour verification, and semester-end reporting.

## Optional editing notes
- If time is tight, shorten the opening and closing first.
- If one workflow is slow, replace it with a cut to the completed result.
- Keep the final video under seven minutes.
