# Feature Wish List

1. **Personalized Dashboard** – Highlight upcoming activities, pending approvals, and unread messages specific to the signed-in volunteer or promoter.
2. **Global Search & Smart Filters** – Combine a search bar with filters (location, category, schedule, required skills) so students can quickly discover relevant activities.
3. **Inline Notifications** – Non-intrusive toast/badge notifications for roster updates, activity state changes, and new comments or messages.
4. **Calendar Mode** – Monthly/weekly calendar showing joined and managed activities with quick tap-to-open actions.
5. **Offline Drafts** – Allow composing activity descriptions or comments offline and automatically sync once connectivity returns.
6. **Media Attachments** – Support images or files on activity cards so organizers can share flyers, checklists, or waivers.
7. **Conversation Inbox** – Dedicated messaging view with threading, read receipts, and typing indicators for channel or direct chats.
8. **Role & Permission Manager** – Visual interface for admins to grant/revoke promoter roles and configure authority scopes without hitting the backend manually.
9. **Activity Insights** – Lightweight analytics (signup trends, completion rates, feedback scores) surfaced to promoters to help them improve events.
10. **Accessibility Controls** – User settings for text scaling, high-contrast themes, and reduced-motion UI to make the app friendlier to all students.
